# Character simulation expansion

All living characters have saved personal records. Monthly reviews are staggered across six cohorts and capped at six decisions per realm per month. Annual estate draws conserve wealth; ordinary adults choose training, ties, personal marriages, personal intrigue, grievances and petitions. Grievances and directed bonds affect quarterly loyalty and therefore canonical factions. Characters remember births, marriages, bereavement and personal decisions. Wards outside royal families learn from eligible guardians; non-ruler AI characters pay for treatment from personal wealth. Player-owned characters are excluded from AI decisions and have explicit personal goals and actions, plus actionable disputes. Existing realm-scale mechanics, claims, succession and plot resolution remain authoritative.

Scope: game abstraction, not CK3 feature parity. Personal relations still use symmetric opinion with an additional directed trust/grievance layer. Health, education, marriage and political schemes remain simplified. Tests cover migration, player control, monthly agency, wealth conservation, ward education, family memories, dilemmas and goal resolution. Browser acceptance and long campaign balance require separate validation.

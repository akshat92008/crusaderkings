# Sovereign 1450 — Commercial Validation & Steam Launch Plan

**Target Storefront:** Steam (Windows PC primary; macOS secondary if hardware-certified)  
**Working Price Hypothesis:** **$14.99** (validated against **$9.99** vs. **$19.99** from external demo willingness-to-pay & perception surveys + fair regional pricing for India, Brazil, China, Germany, Spain)  
**Minimum Cash Budget:** **$100** Steam Direct fee (no paid ads planned; organic + creator outreach).

---

## 1. Product Positioning (No Reference-Stack Tagline)

**Tagline:** *Rule your dynasty. Build your capital. Defend your legacy.*  
**Never Market As:** *"CK2 + Clash of Clans + AoE + Total War."*  

**5 Distinct Pillars Shown in Demo & Store Assets:**
1. A royal capital that physically transforms from frontier camp to imperial fortress.
2. Loyal vs. self-interested Royal Councilors with personal agendas you delegate to at your own risk.
3. Tactile 3D historical regiment battles with simple formation & flank commands.
4. Siege warfare fought across **the exact capital layout you built**, with persistent damage.
5. Multi-generational 1450–1600 succession, Crossroads technology shifts, and dynastic chronicle.

---

## 2. External Alpha & Steam Next Fest Behavioral Gates

Before committing to paid launch, public demo testing (`50–100` unaffiliated strategy/casual/laptop testers → Steam Next Fest cohort) must evaluate real behavior:

| Metric | Poor (Pause & Fix) | Acceptable | Strong Target |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Median Demo Playtime** | `< 15 min` | `25–35 min` | `40+ min` |
| **Would Recommend** | `< 70%` | `80%` | `85%+` |
| **Demo → Wishlist Conversion** | `< 10%` | `15–18%` | `20%+` |
| **7-Day Return Rate** | `< 5%` | `10–15%` | `18%+` |
| **Manual 3D Battle Rate** | `< 15%` | `20–40%` | `40%+` |
| **First-15-Min Abandonment** | `> 15%` | `< 10%` | `< 5%` |

- **Critical Gate Rule:** Steam allows **one** Steam Next Fest entry per game. If the external alpha shows poor onboarding or unstable 3D battles, **do not burn Next Fest early**—hold and target the next eligible Next Fest edition instead of launching an unready demo.

## 3. Marketing, Localization & Legal Gates

1. **Steam Store Package:** Main/small/library capsules, logo, 8–12 genuine in-engine screenshots, 60–90s gameplay trailer (`0–5s` transforming capital → `15–30s` building/army → `30–60s` 3D battle & siege → `60–75s` dynasty succession → `75–90s` wishlist CTA), `~100` strategy creator outreach list.
2. **Localization Priority (Verified Only, Never Machine-Dumped Untested):** English (mandatory), Simplified Chinese (highest upside), German, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian (only if native reviewer verified).
3. **Mandatory Disclosures:** Truthful Steam AI-Content Survey + visible `Wildfire Games / 0 A.D.` (`CC BY-SA 3.0`), `Cliopatria` (`CC BY 4.0`), `Polyfork` (`CC BY 4.0`), and `Bleed`/`Varkalandar` attribution notices.

# Hometown expansion
One shared European-style 2D settlement, 10 town levels, 40 building types and 20 research branches with 10 ranks each. This is a first playable integration, not a claim of final visual fidelity. Existing campaigns migrate lazily when opening Hometown. Existing province farms, markets, barracks and forts are authoritative, not copied. The treasury and food are shared with campaign; wood, stone, iron are separate royal construction reserves. Construction uses campaign months, queues and resources; bulk is best-effort and reports skips. Local lord ownership is not altered; sovereign pays for projects on owned, unoccupied provinces. Existing AI remains unchanged; only realms opened by player adopt new Hometown state. Existing unit specializations and engines remain historically doctrine-gated, now also gated by research once Hometown is opened. No battle renderer changes.
Art: supplied military PNGs (verify original Feudal Wars license for public distribution), City Builder Village Buildings CC0 (see extracted VILLAGE-LICENSE.txt). Run python3 scripts/build_hometown_art.py to extract 23 small selected sheets from existing split archives; package script does this automatically. Static browser builds may use HTTP range reads of exact ZIP members; servers without range support show existing house sprites as fallbacks. Other uploaded packs (terrain, farms, mines, villagers) are not yet classified or integrated. Mine, library and some other buildings use temporary reused art; building levels change scale rather than unique 10-stage architecture. Terrain is procedural. No third-party restricted pack has been unpacked into this change. Verify actual browser/mobile behavior before calling this release-ready.

The town supports plot selection and construction, queued building silhouettes, bulk cost previews, and advancement of campaign months directly from the Hometown screen. Launch scripts extract the village artwork if source archives are present. A separate Hometown browser smoke scenario is appended to the existing browser QA script.

## Expanded presentation and delivery
The 20 research tracks now have 200 named sequential subjects with actual branch modifiers and existing regiment unlocks. These are 200 ranks, not 200 independently simulated effects. The hall has ten composite scenery stages (three original core building appearances plus paving, defenses, banners, towers and other decorations), not ten separately authored meshes. Town Hall includes animated ambient townspeople, carts and smoke; where an optional source cannot be extracted, procedural scenery remains visible. Hometown can recruit ordinary troops at the capital and purchase construction reserves with existing gold. Started construction lost to enemy control is not refunded. The optimized build uses one rotation of each of 23 licensed village designs; selected farmland, cart, mine, villager and terrain assets are included only when the named source archives yield compatible sprites. No restricted Medieval Colony or Stone Mine assets are extracted. The compiler records source members in manifest.json and writes attribution. CI compiles and publishes generated-only art via a normal fast-forward push, and browser/package workflows compile independently. Raw archive files are excluded from the playable ZIP. Real-device tests and manual visual acceptance remain separate from automated checks.

The province inspector now uses the same Hometown quote as construction. The completed scene consumes compiled terrain/road/crop images when they exist. Research exposes all ranks in the UI; siege construction uses royal wood/iron reserves after Hometown activation, with the old local tools/timber mechanism retained for legacy realms. The new progression test exercises all ten hall stages, all forty buildings up to rank ten and all 200 research ranks.

Village buildings use rotation frame 4 (45 degrees) to align with the 2:1 isometric settlement grid; this angle was visually inspected against the supplied sprite sheets.

## Connected agricultural and military loop
Farms add eight base campaign food units per level, with local irrigation/wells, mills, bakeries and reservoirs multiplying production; research benefits all controlled provinces. The campaign food ledger reports gross production, army consumption and net monthly food. Existing merchant grain production uses the same agricultural efficiency, but is a separate inventory. Granaries and warehouses increase local merchant capacity; lumber, quarry, ore and foundry structures increase royal materials, with resource-rich provinces contributing more and occupied provinces excluded. Local cavalry requires a stable; archers require an archery range or level-two barracks. Opening Hometown migrates existing stationed historical troops to basic local training infrastructure once and does not remove troops. Ordinary infantry recruitment remains available. The bulk upgrade dropdown is independent of the town selection. Building cards and province summaries show production effects. No changes to field battle control or enemy war AI.

## Settlement polish
Hometown fits its entire map into the available screen by default, with 100% and 140% zoom modes for precise plot placement. The camera preserves its position across construction updates. Ten composite Town Hall designs and building-rank-dependent annexes use the existing approved sprites; annexes are decorative only. Farm estates visibly expand as farms reach higher levels, with construction progress displayed directly over queued buildings. The browser smoke test now verifies zoom, queue progress, an actual farm upgrade persisted in the campaign save, and captures the settlement separately. Original source artwork is reused; no ten separately authored hall illustrations are claimed.

Self-Audit: Why It Failed
Inflated Scale Mistaken for Cinematic Quality ("Big Donuts")
I scaled Astra radii up by 1.24x–1.38x (280–340px radius, 120–134px ribbon thickness) and wrapped full 270°–300° circles around Rudra.

Procedural Canvas 2D Sine-Wave Textures Hit a Hard Artistic Ceiling
All four elemental ribbons are driven by math-drawn Canvas 2D strips:
Agni: Math.sin() strip + 9 identical Bézier triangles + 12 stamped ellipses → reads like a bright sawblade ring, not roaring painterly fire with translucent smoke washes.
Varuna: 7 parallel ctx.stroke() stripes in flat royal blue + lumpy white circles → reads like a jagged blue tire, not glassy cel-shaded water silk with intricate open-hole Hokusai foam curls and sparkling mist.
Vayu: 4 thick opaque neon-green bands stacked together → reads like rubber green sausages floating in midair, not shredded translucent wind currents and torn-air shards.
Vajra: Thick outlined zigzag strips bunched into a messy knot next to Rudra → reads like tangled cyan-yellow macaroni, not thin, sweeping, high-voltage Zenitsu lightning arcs shooting across the arena with levitating rubble.
Uniform Black Ink Outlines + Opaque Blending = "Sticker" Look
Every ribbon renders at 0.99 opacity with NormalBlending and a uniform black outer border around the entire silhouette.
In Ufotable compositing, fire, lightning, and wind are light sources, not opaque stickers. Glowing fire and lightning do not have a thick uniform black marker outline around their outer edge; they blend through multi-pass additive heat/light glow into dark shadows, with ink accents reserved strictly for internal folds, smoke pockets, and water/foam creases.
Worse, because optical bloom was tied only to GPU UnrealBloomPass (bypassed on software WebGL), the ribbons rendered raw and unbloomed with zero atmospheric light scatter.
Cheap Full-Screen Post-Filters Instead of Real Lighting
The dual_split post-pass painted flat color washes on the left/right edges of the viewport (green/magenta on Vayu, yellow/purple on Vajra) and stamped uniform straight dark-gray radial lines across the whole screen. That looks like an artificial overlay filter, not Ufotable cinematography.
All 4 Elements Share the Same Geometry + Weak Character Integration
Agni, Varuna, and Vayu all spawned variations of the exact same circular ring geometry centered on (x, y), instead of fundamentally different directional shapes tied to Rudra's strike vector.
Rudra stays static in the center without dash/swing afterimage smears, lingering environmental aftermath (scorch/smoke/puddles/cracked stone), or foreground camera-plane parallax embers/mist that give Ufotable frames real 3D depth.
Process Error on My End

# **Crown Monarch — CK3-level completion master plan**

I have checked the current repository, including its complete file tree, major simulation systems, existing implementation documents and latest GitHub verification results.

Our target should no longer be a grand-strategy prototype with features inspired by Crusader Kings. **Crown Monarch must become a complete character-driven grand-strategy game with comparable mechanical depth, meaningful political consequences and a polished interface.**

We should preserve our two differentiators: the worldwide 1450 campaign setting and playable Bannerlord-inspired army battles, without siege or base-building gameplay.

# **3. The complete development plan**

## **Phase 1 — Rebuild the core political architecture**

**Priority: Critical**

Establish one authoritative simulation for characters, houses, titles, governments and armies.

- Individual character wealth, influence, prestige, piety and legitimacy.
- Proper ownership of multiple titles.
- Baron, count, duke, king and emperor equivalents.
- De jure and de facto territories.
- Multiple crowns and personal unions.
- Independent and subordinate rulers using compatible political rules.
- Consistent ownership changes during inheritance, rebellion and conquest.
- Playable subordinate rulers rather than only independent kingdoms.

This is essential because CK3 is fundamentally a game about people holding power, not countries moving armies.

## **Phase 6 — Intrigue and political manipulation**

Build a comprehensive character interaction system with personal, hostile and political schemes.

Include agent recruitment, scheme preparation, intelligence networks, secrets, blackmail, hooks, counterintelligence, bribery, fabrication of evidence, imprisonment, ransom, escape, assassination plots, succession manipulation and faction conspiracies.

Each scheme needs visible risk, progress, potential consequences and reasons for acceptance or rejection.

AI characters must also plot against each other and against the player.

## **Phase 9 — Complete diplomacy and warfare**

Replace overly simple territorial conflicts with a full diplomatic and legal framework.

Include multiple reasons for declaring war, territorial and dynastic claims, independence wars, claimant wars, coalition wars, tributary conflicts, negotiated alliances, calls to war, military access, truces, war contribution, occupation, peace negotiations, reparations and prisoner exchanges.

13

This is where Crown Monarch differentiates itself from Crusader Kings.

Our existing field battles already contain formations, morale, numerical strength calculations, commanders and cultural military doctrines. They need substantial refinement.

The final combat system should include:

- Individual regiment selection and group commands.
- Infantry, cavalry, ranged units and regional specialists.
- Flanking, encirclement, reinforcement and reserves.
- Terrain-dependent combat effectiveness.
- Formation counters, fatigue, morale and routing.
- Commander abilities and leadership consequences.
- Proper numerical superiority and proportional casualties.
- Enemy commanders that react to battlefield conditions.
- Cinematic battlefield presentation and responsive controls.

**No siege gameplay. No Clash of Clans-style base building. Pure strategic army combat.**

A 6,000-versus-300 encounter must reflect the real participating force sizes. A smaller army should only overcome extreme numerical inferiority under genuinely exceptional circumstances—not because our simulation ignores troop counts.

# **5. How I would execute development**

I would use four coordinated development tracks.

Track A · Phases 1–9

**Simulation and political systems**

Track B · Phases 10–12

**Warfare, AI and economy**

Track C · Phases 13–15

**Content and character experiences**

Track D · Phases 16–17

**Interface, map and presentation**

**Integration and final release**

Phase 18 · Testing, balancing and optimization

Instead of repeatedly adding disconnected modules, each development pass must follow the same process: implement, integrate, connect to AI, expose through UI, test, fix regressions and verify before merging.

# **30–40%**

Engineering assessment, not an objectively measured completion score.

We have a substantial playable foundation. We do not need to restart the game or switch engines immediately.

However, matching a mature commercial grand-strategy game with years of expansions is not realistically achievable in one day. We can execute substantial integrated development today, but content quality, historical research, graphics, balancing and extended playtesting cannot be substituted with code generation alone.

**I would keep CK3-level quality as the completion target. I would not lower that target simply to declare the game finished today.**

No code has been modified during this particular audit. I reviewed the complete repository inventory and selected core implementations, but have not independently inspected every source line, asset or live gameplay path. The latest automated checks are green; that does not establish that all gameplay is bug-free.

The 18 phases above are the proposed master roadmap for completing Crown Monarch.
